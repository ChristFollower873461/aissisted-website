Before you buy

Choose the workflow before the configuration.

Start with what you need

Already own one? Send us the model, memory and storage. Haven't bought yet? Wait until we know the job. A Mac mini that runs an agent fine may not have the memory for the local model you want.

Pick one first workflow

Start with a bounded task such as drafting an internal summary from approved documents. We agree its inputs, expected output and review point, then test on public or synthetic material. Connecting every account on day one makes it harder to see what is working.

What we configure

Give your assistant the access it needs.

Separate accounts and approved connections

We set up OpenClaw, the open-source agent, with separate, limited accounts. We agree which folders, tools and services the assistant can reach. You decide what stays disconnected. We distinguish reading from sending or changing records, with human review before customer-facing work leaves the system.

Model choice and logs

We choose where the model runs with you. Some tasks can run locally; others may use a hosted model if that fits your data boundary. Owning the Mac mini does not make every model call local. We review what logs record, who can see them and what should be retained.

After installation

Know what happens when something changes.

Updates and restarts

We agree update and recovery responsibilities, then check how the assistant behaves after a restart. That includes what starts automatically, what needs you to sign in, and how to tell whether the first workflow is running. We show you how to spot a failed connection or update and recover.

Support and practical training

We scope ongoing support, troubleshooting and configuration review with you. Training uses your actual workflow: checking a draft against its source, recognizing a bad result, stopping the assistant and asking for help. We agree what support covers before handoff.

Own the tool

We sell the hammer and teach you to swing it.

Keep control of the setup

The hardware is yours, and so is the setup. We leave you the configuration and written instructions so you can make changes without us. Cloud model providers and connected services bill separately; we show you those costs before you sign up. Read our ownership and local control guide.

Get hands-on help

Work with founder PJ Standley, based in Ocala. Setup and training are available on-site in Ocala and Marion County, or remotely with an agreed access method.
