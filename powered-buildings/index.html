The book

Powered Buildings

How Real Estate Pros Find, Qualify and Broker Small AI Data Center Deals

PJ Standley, B.S. in Engineering Technology · USAII Certified AI Scientist (CAIS™) and Certified AI Consultant (CAIC™)

Companies that run AI computers need electricity more than they need square feet. Some are reported to be looking at smaller sites and older buildings. The old plant, the printing site, the cold store or the telephone building in your market may be worth a second look. Or it may not.

Powered Buildings is a field guide for licensed commercial and industrial real estate agents and brokers. It covers existing buildings and small projects of roughly 1 to 10 megawatts. It shows you how to tell the difference, and how to say plainly what you do not know yet.

It is just as clear about limits. A nameplate is not available power. A nearby substation does not prove capacity. You do not rate a floor. Florida is the worked example, and licensing is state law. No hype. No promised deals.
