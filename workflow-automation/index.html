Questions

What owners usually ask first.

What can a small business automate?

Common starting points include intake, missed-call follow-up, scheduling reminders, quote follow-up, CRM updates, document routing, recurring reports, and owner review. The right first workflow is repeated, visible, and still has a clear human decision point.

Does workflow automation require AI?

No. Some workflows improve through a simpler form, rule, integration, or checklist. AI is useful when the work includes language, summaries, classification, or context, but it should be added only where it makes the process clearer.

Can AIssisted Consulting automate tools a business already uses?

Yes. AIssisted Consulting starts with the current process and can connect forms, calendars, messages, spreadsheets, CRM records, booking, payments, and reporting when the tools and access support a reliable integration.
