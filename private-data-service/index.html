How it works

Six steps and one written receipt at the end.

The first pass is small enough to inspect. You see the mapping rules and the verification sample before anything runs at scale.

1

Intake

You hand us an encrypted drive or use a secure upload. Data lands in your own isolated container.

2

Mapping

We review a sample with you, agree on the target format and cleanup rules, and build a small graded verification set.

3

Processing

Records run through our private system. The processing machine has no internet access.

4

QA

Automated scoring against the verification set, then a human spot-check.

5

Delivery

Clean data in your system's format, a one-page fix report, and an integrity receipt.

6

Monthly, optional

New and changed records get the same treatment each month with a short change report.
