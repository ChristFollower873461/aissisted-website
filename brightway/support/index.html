Common questions

How do I unlock Parent Zone?

Open BrightWay and choose Parent Zone. Enter the six-digit PIN created when the learner profile was set up. Parent Zone protects profile settings, workshop limits, provider choices, optional AI consent, and account controls.

How do I permanently delete an account?
  1. Sign in with the parent account.
  2. Unlock Parent Zone.
  3. Open Account and find Delete this BrightWay account.
  4. Enter the account password, type DELETE, and confirm.

This removes the parent account, learner profile, usage records, generated mission pictures, and an active billing customer if one exists. The action cannot be undone. You may also email support for help with a deletion request.

Why can I not open a mission?

BrightWay needs an internet connection to screen a question and prepare a mission. Confirm the device is online, then pull down to refresh or choose Try again. If the problem continues, include the device model, iOS version, and approximate time in your support email. Do not include a child's full name or other unnecessary personal information.

How do I print a mission sheet?

Open the mission and choose the print action. On iPhone or iPad, BrightWay opens the native print panel. Select an available AirPrint printer or use the system share options to save the mission sheet.

What does BrightWay send to AI providers?

Solo mode sends the screened question, age band, and parent-selected workshop track to the provider chosen by the parent. Squad and generated mission illustrations are off by default and each requires separate parent consent. The privacy policy explains the minimized data sent for those optional features.

Is billing open?

No. BrightWay billing is currently unavailable. The iOS app does not sell a digital subscription or send families to a web checkout.
