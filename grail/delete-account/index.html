Grail account and data deletion

Delete your Grail account and data.

Use this page to ask AIssisted Consulting LLC to close your Grail account or workspace and delete the associated data. You can submit the request here even if you no longer have the app.

Submit a deletion request

No sign-in is required. Never send a password, access code, or private customer data.

Request deletion

Use the email address associated with Grail so we can identify the correct account or workspace. We may reply to verify ownership before completing the request.

We normally acknowledge requests within three business days and complete verified requests within 30 days.

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge the request and identify the Grail account or workspace.
  2. We verify ownership through the associated email when necessary.
  3. We close the workspace, delete associated data, and confirm completion.

Active service or subscription

An account-deletion request also asks us to close the related Grail workspace. If an active paid service exists, we will include its cancellation and offboarding in the process. You do not need to submit a separate deletion request.

What is deleted

  • The Grail account or managed workspace, if one exists.
  • Contact, onboarding, campaign, proof, and workspace data associated with that account.
  • Associated data held by service providers where deletion applies.

Local app data

Grail working data saved only on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Limited retention

We may retain records required for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legal obligations. Backup copies may remain until the normal backup cycle overwrites them. Retained data is limited to the required purpose and period.

Email option: You may instead email pj@aissistedconsulting.com from the address associated with Grail. Use the subject “Grail account deletion request.”
