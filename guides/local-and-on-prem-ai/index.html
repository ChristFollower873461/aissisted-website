Small business guide

Local, private, and on-prem AI

Small businesses do not have to choose between using capable AI and keeping control of sensitive work. Start by deciding where each task runs, what data may leave, and who approves the result.

Practical first step

Draw the data boundary before choosing a model.

List the inputs, where processing happens, what gets retained, and which actions still require a person.

Four practical choices

Choose the boundary that fits the workflow.

Cloud AI

A hosted provider runs the model. It is often the fastest route to strong capabilities, but the business should review data use, retention, access, and vendor terms before connecting sensitive work.

Local AI

A model runs on a business-controlled computer or device. This can reduce outside data movement, though the machine, software, permissions, updates, and logs still need active management.

On-prem AI

Processing stays on business-managed infrastructure. It offers more operational control, while making the business responsible for hardware, availability, security, backups, and maintenance.

Hybrid AI

Sensitive records stay inside a controlled boundary while lower-risk or high-compute tasks use hosted models. The split must be written clearly enough to test and audit.

Decision guide

When local or on-prem AI may be worth it.

Consider it when

The workflow uses sensitive internal context, needs to work with limited connectivity, has stable repeated tasks, or requires tighter control over where information is processed.

Pause when

The business lacks someone to maintain the system, the task needs a frontier model's capabilities, or the local hardware and support cost outweigh the value of the workflow.

Use hybrid when

Records can be filtered or summarized locally before an approved, lower-risk portion reaches a hosted service. Keep the boundary visible and test that it behaves as designed.

Before go-live

A local system still needs guardrails.

Map every connection

Document the files, apps, APIs, users, and devices the workflow can reach. Local processing does not help if an integration quietly exports the same data.

Limit and retain deliberately

Give the workflow only what it needs. Set practical rules for prompts, outputs, logs, backups, deletion, and access instead of relying on the server location alone.

Keep a human checkpoint

Require approval for customer-facing messages, financial changes, permission changes, sensitive records, and exceptions the workflow was not designed to resolve.

Common questions

Local AI, in plain English.

What is local AI?

AI processing that runs on a computer or device the business controls instead of sending every task to a hosted model.

Is on-prem automatically private?

No. Placement matters, but so do permissions, logs, retention, backups, integrations, network access, and day-to-day operating practices.

Is hybrid a compromise?

Often it is the practical design: keep sensitive context inside while using hosted capabilities only for a defined, lower-risk part of the task.
