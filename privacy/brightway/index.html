1. Scope and current service

This policy explains how BrightWay AI ("BrightWay," "we," "us") handles information when a parent or guardian creates an account and a learner uses BrightWay under that account.

BrightWay is currently an invitation-only, parent-supervised private beta for families with learners ages 5-12. The beta includes data-minimization and parent controls. It is not a claim that every legal or operational requirement for an unrestricted public children's service has been completed.

Plain-language summary: BrightWay has no ads, public profiles, social feed, or third-party tracking. We do not sell personal information. Parents control the learner profile, optional AI features, and account deletion.

Information BrightWay does not request

BrightWay does not request a learner's phone number, precise location, contacts, social-media account, microphone recording, camera image, or full legal name. Do not enter unnecessary personal information in a question.

Local device storage

Recent questions, answers, mission state, and selected preferences may be stored in the app's private website data on the device so the family can continue the workshop. BrightWay's application database does not keep raw question and answer logs in the current beta.

Network abuse prevention

BrightWay derives a short-lived security token from the client network identifier using a keyed cryptographic hash. The raw network address is not retained in BrightWay's rate-limit records, and the token is not used for advertising or cross-service tracking.

Billing

Billing is disabled in the current private beta and the iOS app does not collect payment information. If paid service is introduced later, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that collection begins.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate the parent and operate the family account.
  • Create screened, age-matched explanations and hands-on missions.
  • Apply workshop limits, topic settings, and parent-selected modes.
  • Provide optional mission illustrations after separate parent consent.
  • Protect BrightWay and families from abuse, fraud, unsafe content, and unauthorized access.
  • Respond to support, privacy, correction, export, and deletion requests.
  • Maintain reliability and comply with legal or store obligations.

4. Third-party AI processing

BrightWay uses third-party AI providers to generate explanations and missions. AI output can be incomplete or inaccurate. Parents should supervise use and review the written safety notes before a learner begins an activity.

Solo mode

Solo mode sends the screened question, age band, and parent-selected workshop track to the AI provider selected by the parent. Depending on deployment configuration, the available providers may include OpenAI, Anthropic, Moonshot AI, or xAI.

Squad mode

Squad is off by default and requires a separate parent opt-in. When enabled, the limited question context is sent to the available participating providers. BrightWay screens their summaries and sends the original question plus screened summaries to OpenAI to assemble the final mission. A parent may turn Squad off in Parent Zone.

Mission illustrations

Generated mission illustrations are off by default and require their own parent opt-in. BrightWay sends OpenAI only the already-screened mission title, materials, and steps. It does not include the raw learner question, learner name, profile, parent email, chat history, or provider transcript in the image request.

BrightWay enables optional provider features only when their reviewed privacy and retention requirements are explicitly configured. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy commitments.

5. How we share information

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Information may be shared only as needed with:

  • AI providers described above, according to the parent's selected mode and consent.
  • Infrastructure and security providers that host, protect, or deliver the service.
  • Support providers when a parent requests help.
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, necessary to protect safety or rights, or connected to a lawful business transfer.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Parent and learner account data remains while the account is active and is removed when the account is deleted, subject to limited legal or security obligations.
  • Raw questions and answers shown in recent history remain in local app storage and are not kept as application-database chat logs in the current beta.
  • An illustration job may temporarily hold the minimized mission title, materials, and steps for no more than 15 minutes while active. The prompt is cleared when the job becomes ready, blocked, or failed.
  • Generated illustration bytes remain in authenticated active storage for no more than seven days and no more than 24 ready illustrations per account. Revoking illustration consent deletes the account's active illustration records.
  • Operational database backups are retained for no more than seven days. A deleted record may remain in a protected backup until that backup expires.
  • Short-lived security and rate-limit records are pruned as they expire.

7. Parent choices and privacy controls

A parent or guardian may:

  • Review or update the learner profile and workshop settings.
  • Choose the available AI provider, Quick or Deep setting, and Solo or Squad mode.
  • Decline or revoke Squad and illustration consent independently.
  • Request access, correction, or export of account information.
  • Permanently delete the account and associated family data from the Account screen after unlocking Parent Zone and confirming the account password.

For help with any privacy choice, email pj@aissistedconsulting.com.

8. Children's privacy

BrightWay is designed for a learner to use only under a parent or guardian account. The current private beta uses an invitation code, parent password confirmation, a Parent Zone PIN, and specific processing notices before a learner profile is created.

This controlled beta flow is not represented as a substitute for every form of verifiable parental consent that may be required for a public launch. If you believe a learner supplied personal information outside the parent-account context, contact us so we can investigate, delete it where appropriate, and prevent further collection.

9. Security, international processing, and changes

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

BrightWay is operated from the United States. Information may be processed in the United States or other locations where a selected provider operates.

We may update this policy as BrightWay changes. Material updates will appear here with a revised effective date, and we will take reasonable steps to notify parent account holders when appropriate.
